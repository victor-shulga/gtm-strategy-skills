# Growth planner interview

Ask block by block, one block at a time, in the owner's language. Each question says what is needed,
where it usually lives, and what to do when there is no data. Whatever you already pulled from a source,
state it and ask only for confirmation ("the P&L shows X, correct?"). Do not ask for anything a CRM,
P&L or calendar can answer.

Rule for "I don't know": set an assumption, mark it as dashed in the planner, add it to the list
"replace after the first month". Never invent Point A.

---

## Block 1. Goals and horizon

1. **Revenue since the start of the year?** Where: P&L or accounting, revenue by month. Closed months only.
2. **The plan for this year?** Where: financial model, owner's decision, partner deck. No plan: write "none", the yardstick becomes last year plus growth.
3. **Revenue target for next year?** Where: the owner. Prompt: name the number you would not be embarrassed by and the number that scares you. Take the first, record the second as the ambitious scenario.
4. **Start month and horizon.** The engine plans Q4 of this year plus the four quarters of next year, so the start month is October.

## Block 2. Current clients

5. **Who pays now and how much per month?** Where: last month's P&L, invoices, CRM ("Client" stage). Format: client · $/month · contract end date if any.
6. **Whose contract ends, and who is at risk?** Where: contracts, client-health notes, the account manager's read. A client at risk gets a 50% chance.
7. **How many clients leave per month on average?** Where: 12 months of P&L (who disappeared). Formula: 1 ÷ average client lifetime in months. No data: 8%.
8. **Deals being signed right now?** Where: CRM "Proposal" / "Signing" stages. Each one goes in with a chance.

## Block 3. Offers and prices

9. **What exactly do new clients buy?** Where: pricing page, proposals in the CRM, P&L by service. Format: offer · price · per month or one-off.
10. **How many months does a client really pay for each offer?** Where: P&L (first and last payment per client), contract dates in the CRM. The minimum contract is the floor.
11. **What share of new deals goes to each offer?** Where: deals closed last year and this year. Check: the average deal value should be close to the actual LTV from the P&L.

## Block 4. Point A (last year + closed quarters of this year)

Take the whole of last year plus the closed quarters of this one: a service business closes few deals,
and half a year is too short to measure conversion. Keep the last 2 quarters separately to compare fresh
pace with the average.

12. **How many first (intro) calls in the window?** Where: booking calendar (Calendly, TidyCal and similar), call recorder, CRM deals that reached the "Meeting" stage.
13. **Where did each call come from?** Where: the source field in the CRM, the booking form, the seller's memory. Anything unattributed goes to a separate "inbound and other" row.
14. **How many new paying clients, and from which channel?** Where: P&L (first payment) plus the CRM source. Count by first payment, not by CRM status. Do not count sponsorships, referral payouts, or clients who came through a personal acquaintance (warm exceptions).
15. **How much activity did each channel have?** Where:
    - LinkedIn content: profile analytics or a post tracker, number of posts.
    - LinkedIn outreach: the outreach platform or Sales Navigator (invitations sent).
    - Cold email: the sending platform (new recipients, not emails).
    - Freelance marketplaces: bids and invites from the platform log.
    - Referrals, events, partners: usually nobody counts them, so this becomes an assumption.
16. **How many proposals were sent?** Where: CRM "Proposal" stage, the proposals folder. Needed only for hours.

## Block 5. Channels for next year

17. **Which channels stay, which are new, which close?**
18. **Who owns each channel?** One person per channel. A target without an owner is a wish.
19. **How many actions per week can the owner really do?** Ask the owner, not the CEO.
20. **What do the channel's tools cost per month?** Where: invoices, the company card.

## Block 6. Upsells

21. **How many upsells last year, and for how much?** Where: P&L (income type "upsell"), CRM deal type.
22. **What can be sold to current clients, and how many per quarter?** Format: count per quarter · $/month · months.
23. **Who owns current clients and upsells?**

## Block 7. Products sold without a call (courses, templates, DIY)

24. **Which products sell without a call, and when are the next launches?** Where: the product page, the launch calendar.
25. **How many people are on the waitlist, and which tiers did they pick?** Where: the waitlist table or form.
26. **How did the last such product sell?** Buyers, average price, waitlist conversion. This is Point A for the product.
27. **How many hours does running one cohort take?**

## Block 8. Team and capacity

28. **How many hours per week does each person really give to sales?** What they give now, not what they should.
29. **How many clients can the team carry at once without hiring?**
30. **What is an hour worth for the cost calculation?** Salary ÷ hours, or the owner's rate.
31. **How many days from the first call to payment?** Where: CRM (deal created → deal won). Convert to months and round.

## Block 9. Plan review

Show the filled planner and walk four questions:
- **Gap to target:** close it with more activity, pricier offers, or a lower target?
- **Capacity:** if the hours do not fit, what gets cut or who gets hired, and when?
- **Client ceiling:** when do you cross it, and what then (price, hiring, a product instead of a service)?
- **Single-channel dependence:** if one channel brings more than half the deals, which second channel gets built up?

After the decisions, fix each owner's numbers (the "team KPIs" block) and carry them into the weekly report.
